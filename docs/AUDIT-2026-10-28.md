### F-1 · The Data Omnibus Council mandate (P0)

**The primary evidence.** ST 14756/26, on the Council's register:
https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-14756-2026-INIT/en/pdf (179 pages, PDF created 22 Oct
2026, not marked LIMITE). Dated *Brussels, 22 October 2026*. It is an **OUTCOME OF PROCEEDINGS** from the
General Secretariat to Delegations, interinstitutional file 2025/0360 (COD), previous document 14532/26.
Its subject is the Digital Omnibus, "- Mandate for negotiations with the European Parliament". Cover text:
*"Delegations will find in the Annex the text of the mandate for negotiations with the European
Parliament on the above-mentioned file as agreed by the Permanent Representatives Committee at its
meeting on 21 October 2026."*

**What it establishes, exactly.** **Coreper agreed** the mandate on **21 October**, and the document
recording that is dated **22 October**. The lead's "Council negotiating mandate dated 22 October 2026"
is the document's date, not the date of agreement. A negotiating mandate is the Council's position for
trilogues. It is not a Council first-reading position and not adoption, and nothing in the proposal is
law. The document does not record how delegations voted, and no Council press release could be read
(HTTP 403), so the site says nothing about the vote.

**Parliament, re-read the same day.** Legislative Observatory, 2025/0360(COD): stage reached is still
*Awaiting committee decision*. The only key events are the proposal (19/11/2025) and the committee
referral (19/01/2026), and the documentation gateway is unchanged. So no trilogue can have begun, which
now rests on Parliament alone.

**What the site said, and how wrong it was.**

| Where | Text (until this commit) | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `index.html` Part VIII chip `part-8.span3` (+ es/fr/it) | "Data Omnibus: proposal, no Council mandate" | **False since 21 Oct**: undated, present tense |
| `index.html` `part-8.li2` (+ es/fr/it) | "…has no Council mandate: successive Presidency texts have not yet reached an agreed position." | **False since 21 Oct** |
| `index.html` `part-8.p4` (+ es/fr/it) | "…no outcome of that meeting had been published when this was checked on 21 October 2026 … As at 21 October 2026 no Council mandate has been reported…" | Dated and true when checked; **stale** from 22 Oct |
| `data/instruments.json` `data-omnibus.status_note` | "with no Council negotiating mandate reported and no Parliament position as at 21 October 2026"; the 21 Oct meeting "whose outcome had not been published when checked that day" | Dated, **stale** |
| `clm-data-omnibus-coreper-21-october` | "…no outcome of that meeting had been published when this was checked on 21 October 2026" | Dated, **stale**. Its own note said it "must be replaced the moment an outcome is published" |
| `tools/selftest.mjs` O4 | asserted the chip text "no Council mandate" | **The test pinned the error** |

**Root cause.** The fault is in the **canonical data and the prose**, not the pipeline. The generated
pages and production faithfully rendered what `data/` and `index.html` said. On 21 Oct the record was
right: the outcome was not yet published. The state changed the next day. Nothing re-reads a source on
its own, and the claim's only safeguard was its note's instruction to re-verify before every
publication. Two places made it worse. The chip and the bullet were **undated** present-tense
statements, so they became false the moment Coreper agreed. And a test froze the chip's wording as a
fact (O4). F-3 is the part a check could have done better, and §4 says why no interval would have
caught this one.

**The correction** (§3) uses the existing workflow: a source record for the document actually read, a
new claim, the superseded half of the dated claim replaced as its note required, the status note and
`status_as_of` re-dated, the prose and the three locale overlays, and regeneration through
`node tools/_footer.mjs`. **`legislative_status` stays `status:proposal`**, which remains true (see F-4).

### F-3 · The institutional-status review interval

`docs/EVIDENCE-MODEL.md` §8 and `docs/CONTENT-FRESHNESS-POLICY.md` put **institutional status** in the
**high** class, with a 30-day review. `js/evidence-model.js riskClass()` gave every claim the medium class
(90 days) unless it was linked to a critical enforcement record. **What the Council or Parliament has
done on a file still in procedure** was reviewed as a general fact. Seven claims move: the six Data
Omnibus Council and Parliament claims and the new one. A law-type claim about a proposal
(`clm-cloud-ai-act-dual-basis`) and the history of an adopted act (`clm-ai-omnibus-legislative-path`)
do not.

**What it does not do.** No interval would have caught F-1. The record was read on 21 Oct and the
state changed on 22 Oct, so it was *fresh* under either class (P3 asserts exactly this). Freshness is
an age, not a verification. `freshness.mjs` fetches nothing and cannot see that a source changed. A
fresh record is not a true one. What this change buys is that a pending-procedure claim reaches the
work list at 30 days rather than 90. On this tree, that surfaces `clm-data-omnibus-stalled` (read 26 Sep,
secondary sources) as *review due*. It was not stamped: it was not re-read.

### F-4 · `status:council-position` has no place in the status displays (open)

`data/taxonomy.json` has `status:council-position` ("Council position or general approach"). It is
now the narrower true stage of the Data Omnibus. Setting it was tried and reverted, because the site
cannot show it:

- `js/masthead.js` `BANDS` has no band for it. The Data Omnibus is `scope:core`, so the home page's
  "Where the core rulebook stands" would count it as **"1 with no status recorded"**, a false statement.
- `js/instrument-view.js statusBadge()` maps only `proposal`, `stalled` and the post-adoption states,
  so its badge falls to `neutral`, not `provisional`. This already applies to `psd3` and `psr`
  (`status:political-agreement`, `scope:referenced`).
- `style.css` (`.sop-seg`, `.sop-band`, `.tk-item`), `css/evidence.css` (`.is-pill`) and
  `css/tools.css` (`.ap-status`) style only `proposal` and `stalled` among the pre-adoption states.

**For the author.** Either leave the scalar at `status:proposal` (true, coarser), or add the
pre-adoption stages to those displays first (a frontend change: a band's wording is the author's) and
then move the record. The status note says which was chosen and why.
