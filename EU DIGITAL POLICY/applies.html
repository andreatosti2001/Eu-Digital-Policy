What applies to me?

Three questions, answered against — rules held as data. Every answer shows the rule that produced it, what that rule turns on, and where it comes from. Where nothing covers your combination, the tool says so rather than guessing.

Read this before you read a result

This is a map of the rulebook, not legal advice, and it cannot become legal advice however many boxes you tick. It asks three questions where a lawyer would ask fifty, and applicability in the real cases usually turns on the facts it does not ask for: your exact service architecture, your contracts, your sector, your Member State, your size over three financial years, and whether a Commission decision has actually been taken about you.

So the answers are graded. Yes means the instrument applies on the facts you gave. Potentially applicable means it turns on something unestablished — and the result tells you what. Probably not is the weakest of the three: an obligation absent here is not a safe harbour. And where no rule matches at all you get Not determined, never a negative finding.
